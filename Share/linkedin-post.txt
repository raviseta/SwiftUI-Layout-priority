What does .layoutPriority(1) actually change in SwiftUI?

I built a small Layout Lab to make the answer visible.

Three experiments, one modifier:
• HStack: watch flexible blocks compete for limited width.
• ZStack: let the text determine the stack’s size.
• Custom Layout: use priority to change order while keeping equal widths.

The useful comparison: switch between 1 and 100. In these examples, the result is the same. Priority expresses relative order, not a size multiplier.

The demo includes live size readouts, a width slider, and code snippets. The video shows it running in the iOS Simulator.
